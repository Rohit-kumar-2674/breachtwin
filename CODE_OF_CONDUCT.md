# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different levels of experience, give specific technical feedback, and evaluate ideas with reproducible evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintain discussion around the project's purpose. Report concerning behavior privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community. No response-time guarantee is implied.
